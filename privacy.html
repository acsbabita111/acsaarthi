ACSAARTHI™Privacy & Study Data
Privacy

Employer responses are evidence, not publicity material.

The study is designed to publish aggregated learning while protecting respondent-specific information.

What may be collected

Organisation profile, respondent role/contact information, driver categories, operational pain points, skill gaps, hiring ranges, future skill needs, refresher priorities and assessment preferences.

What may be published

Aggregated counts, percentages, ranges and themes after quality review and minimum-sample checks.

What is not published by default

Individual responses, respondent contact details, company-specific hiring numbers or attributed comments without explicit permission.

Purpose

To support evidence-based competency, curriculum, assessment and workforce-demand design for the ACSAARTHI initiative.

Before the employer survey goes live, this page should be updated with the final data controller/contact wording, retention period and the exact survey platform/Firebase workflow.
