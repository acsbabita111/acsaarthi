What We Ask Employers

Ten questions tied to project indicators.

Current operations

  • Driver categories employed
  • Recurring driver-related problems
  • Business impact of those problems
  • Skill gaps in new hires
  • Hard-to-fill driver roles

Future workforce

  • Approximate annual driver hiring
  • Replacement vs business-growth hiring
  • Future must-have driver skills
  • Refresher/upskilling priorities
  • What employers verify before hiring
No money ask. The employer study is designed to learn from operational experience, not to solicit funding or sell a predetermined training solution.

Methodology

Evidence first. Claims later.

1. Listen

Collect employer pain points, hiring demand, future skills and refresher needs through a short structured survey.

2. Validate

Review response quality, compare sectors and geographies, and follow up where clarification is needed.

3. Design

Use validated demand evidence to shape competencies, curriculum, assessment and later equipment requirements.

Publication rules

  • No company is linked to a specific response without explicit permission.
  • Every public percentage must show or be traceable to its sample size.
  • Very small samples are not presented as broad market conclusions.
  • Every finding carries an update date and may change as the evidence base grows.
