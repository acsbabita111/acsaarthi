Why ACSAARTHI™

Professional driving is changing.

Road safety, advanced vehicle technology, digital fleet systems, customer expectations, specialised vehicles and new mobility models are changing what employers expect from a professional driver. ACSAARTHI™ is being built around evidence from the field.

01

Understand

Listen to driver-employing organisations and identify real operational problems and workforce gaps.

02

Define

Translate recurring needs into clear skills, competencies and assessment priorities.

03

Prepare

Use validated evidence to shape future training, refresher learning and workforce readiness.
